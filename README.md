## How to Use:

1. Join the discord servers from link https://discord.gg/YZ4ec83nKM\
2. All posible commands are on channel linked here: https://discord.com/channels/1543539574603784224/1543539576142827572 or below.
3. Go to channel https://discord.com/channels/1543539574603784224/1543539576142827573 and write your first command!
## All commands

- /d2 - A roll of a two-sided die.
- /d4 - A roll of a four-sided die.
- /d6 - A roll of a six-sided die.
- /d10 - A roll of a ten-sided die.
- /d20 - A roll of a twenty-sided die.
- /d100 - A roll of a hundred-sided die.
- /roll <number> - A roll with a custom number of sides.
